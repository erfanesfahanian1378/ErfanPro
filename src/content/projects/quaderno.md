title: Quaderno
summary: A self-hostable study notebook for language learners. Class handouts in any format become annotatable PDFs, you can write your own pages between them, and it shows how many hours you really studied.
kind: personal
period: 2026
date: 2026-09-12
role: Personal project · open source (MIT)
tags: [Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Prisma, PWA, Docker]
highlights:
  - Converts PDFs, Word files, slide decks and images to PDF on upload, with OCR for scanned handouts, and always keeps the original file byte-identical.
  - An annotation layer with highlights, a freehand pen with pressure and palm rejection, threaded comments and your own note pages inserted anywhere, exported as flattened or layered PDFs.
  - Study tracking with a live timer, weekly goals, charts, a year heatmap and streaks, plus SM-2 spaced-repetition vocabulary review with Anki export.
  - An installable, offline-tolerant PWA built to run on a €5/month VPS, with a separate worker for conversion and OCR and PostgreSQL doubling as the job queue.
